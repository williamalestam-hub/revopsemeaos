                                          What isn't counted

                                          • Calls: about 175 logged in 4 weeks by 15 people, nearly all dialled through Outreach and mostly unanswered. Call result, type and duration are hidden from EMEA profiles.
                                          • LinkedIn: only Outreach LinkedIn steps are logged (about 700 in 4 weeks, by 4 BDRs, since September 2026).
                                          • Emails sent only through Outreach that mailbox capture didn't copy: about 1 in 8 of the people Outreach emailed in 4 weeks had no mailbox copy, more for SMB and Engagement Managers, so emails read somewhat low.
                                          • Replies and email quality: received mail is left out.
                                          • Meetings that aren't on the Salesforce calendar (in person, phone) or are with people who aren't a contact or lead in Salesforce; no-shows and declined invites; what happened in a meeting.
                                          • Who booked a meeting for whom, such as a BDR booking for an AE.
                                          • Time off: Quiet may be a holiday.

                                          How it's counted

                                          • Last 4 weeks: the 28 days to yesterday. The 4 weeks before: days 29–56 ago. Next 2 weeks: the 14 days from tomorrow. All in your Salesforce time zone, so today's activity shows tomorrow.
                                          • Emails sent: email tasks with a sent Email Message, which is Einstein Activity Capture's copy of the seller's mailbox, dated by the email's date. Outreach sends count because they go out from the mailbox. Received mail, Outreach's own email log, automation emails with no captured message and RevOps admin tasks are left out. People and accounts emailed are distinct per seller; team figures add them up per seller.
                                          • External meetings: calendar records with a contact, lead or account (not Legora's own), not all-day, owned by the seller, whoever created them. One meeting per start time per person (repeats are found within one account status, so a copy of the meeting on a record with another status, about 1–2% of meetings, still counts again); LeanData BookIt meetings marked Canceled or Rescheduled are left out. A meeting with two of our people counts for each of them, and a meeting on the calendar counts as held.
                                          • Customers and prospects: by the account's status today. Customer counts as a customer; everything else, contacts with no account and leads included, counts as a prospect.
                                          • Changes are like-for-like: only current sellers and leaders who joined 8+ weeks ago, whose capture was on in the first week of the earlier window and who have something captured in the last 4 weeks (sellers with a capture gap are left out too). Mailbox and calendar capture were switched on for most sellers between May and October 2026, so longer comparisons would measure the rollout, not selling.
                                          • Activity follows Salesforce sharing. If your access shows activity for fewer than half the sellers, empty rows read Not visible.
